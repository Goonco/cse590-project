\begin{abstract}
NetKAT provides an algebraic foundation for expressing and reasoning about network policies. However, its simplified packet model does not inherently capture protocol-dependent field relationships or application-specific packet invariants. Consequently, policies that are well-defined under NetKAT's semantics may still violate assumptions about packet structure, particularly through packet modifications and policy composition. To address these limitations, we propose Typed NetKAT, a schema-aware extension of NetKAT that integrates user-defined packet structures and constraints into static policy checking. First, we introduce a declarative packet abstraction that supports nominal field types and field dependencies independently of frontend-specific APIs. Second, we develop a compositional refinement type system that tracks how NetKAT operators transform packet invariants and verifies the compatibility of policy input and output contracts. Typed NetKAT translates well-typed policies into standard NetKAT through type erasure, enabling reuse of existing compilation infrastructure. We plan to implement a prototype and evaluate its schema extensibility, static error detection, and compilation compatibility.
\end{abstract}
